Questions

Straight answers.

Where does our data live?

In your firm’s own Microsoft 365. Client records, documents and approvals stay in your tenancy.

Does anything happen without a person?

No. Bunya prepares the work, and nothing is used or sent until someone on your team checks it.

Do we have to replace the system we use now?

No. Start with Command Centre, add only the pieces your firm needs, and keep the tools that already work for you. We look at what your firm uses now and agree what to keep, connect or replace.

Can we build our own tools?

Yes. With Bunya Shape, included with every Bunya, you describe an app or agent in plain English and it’s built on your own client record. Nothing goes live until someone on your team approves it.

What’s Care?

Support and updates. It’s included when you subscribe, and priced separately if you buy Command Centre outright.
