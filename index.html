The paperwork keeps growing.

Australian advice firms are asked to do more for every client, with fewer advisers to do it.

More paperwork for every client

File notes, fact finds, SOAs, records of advice, annual reviews and a fee consent renewal every year. Each client brings more of it than ever.

Fewer advisers, more people needing advice

About 15,400 advisers serve the whole country, so every hour an adviser spends on admin is an hour a client waits.

Systems that don’t talk to each other

A CRM, spreadsheets, a shared drive and email threads. The same client details typed in again and again.

Common questions.

Where does our client data live?

In your firm’s own Microsoft 365. Client records, documents and approvals stay in your tenancy, and Bunya works with Teams, Outlook and SharePoint.

Who decides what goes to clients?

Your team. Bunya prepares the work, and a person checks it, changes what they want and decides what’s used or sent.

What about our licensee?

If your firm is self-licensed, you can start straight away. If you’re with a licensee, we ask them to approve Bunya first, with the Licensee Green-Light Pack.

Do we have to replace everything at once?

No. Start with Command Centre, add only the features your firm needs, and keep the tools that already work for you.

What does getting started involve?

Standard setup, $7,249 once: we install Bunya in your Microsoft 365, move your clients across and train your team, and a named engineer stays beside you for 30 days after go-live, or 45 with the Advice Engine.

Can we change our Bunya later?

Yes. Add or remove features and apps as your firm changes. New features are offered, never forced. With Bunya Shape, you can change your forms and reports, or build your own apps and agents, in plain English.
