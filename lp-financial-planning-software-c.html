Every question, answered.

Where does our client data live?

In your firm’s own Microsoft 365. Client records, documents and approvals stay in your tenancy, and Bunya works with Teams, Outlook and SharePoint.

Does the AI give advice or send anything by itself?

No. Bunya prepares drafts: file notes, fact finds, review packs and, in pilot, SOAs. A person on your team checks and approves every one before it’s used or sent.

We’re with a licensee. Is that a problem?

No. We ask your licensee to approve Bunya before your team uses it with clients. If they don’t, your deposit is refunded in full.

What happens to our history?

Choose to start clean, bring your active clients across, or bring the full history. There’s a trial run before the real move.

Do we have to replace everything at once?

No. Start with Command Centre, add only the features your firm needs, and keep the tools that already work for you.

How is it priced?

Per client household, from $3 a month, and a couple counts as one. Apps are a flat monthly price for the firm. Microsoft bills its own licences directly.

How do we get the team using it?

Setup includes a Team Quick-Start Kit and a named engineer beside you for 30 days after go-live, or 45 with the Advice Engine. Care adds tutorials for each role and a team to ask.

Can we change it as the firm changes?

Yes. With Bunya Shape, change your forms and reports, or build your own apps and agents, in plain English. Our build team handles the bigger jobs.

Is the SOA generator ready?

It’s in pilot with early firms. The rest of the Advice Engine, from the fact find to modelling, is available now.
