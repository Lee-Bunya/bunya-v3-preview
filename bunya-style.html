Principles

Six rules. Everything else follows.

  • 01Space first

    White and pale grey carry the page. One idea per screen, centred, with room to breathe.

  • 02Show the work

    Prove it with the document, the screen, the moment of approval. Not adjectives, not invented numbers.

  • 03Teal speaks, gold says yes

    Teal is the only accent. Gold appears only where a person on the team has approved something.

  • 04The diamond is ours

    A piece of the Bunya mark, used for the pattern, the bullets, the labels and the loading state.

  • 05One wow per page

    Each page gets one staged moment: a dark band, a film, a big reveal. Everything else stays calm.

  • 06Calm motion

    Things settle into place. Nothing spins, bounces or flies across the screen, except the gold diamond landing.

The gold rule

Gold is the yes.

Gold means one thing: a named person on your team approved this. If it appears anywhere else, it stops meaning anything. Over time, visitors learn that gold is the yes, which is the whole promise of the product.

Use gold for

  • The approval chip  Approved 10:42
  • The diamond landing at the end of the signature
  • The last step in “Prepared, checked, approved”

Never for

  • Buttons, links or highlights
  • Gradients in headlines
  • Decoration, icons or backgrounds

Imagery

Show the work, not people.

Do

  • Stand-in screens, large and crisp, in a soft window on the diamond grid.
  • Short, silent recordings of the stand-in screens doing one thing.
  • The Bunya Mountains, the pines and the cone as texture, in one band per page.
  • Documents and approval chips as the proof.

Don’t

  • Faces, staged office photos or anything that looks like a real client.
  • The real Command Centre interface, until we say it can be shown.
  • Invented numbers. If a figure is illustrative, label it.
  • Gold used as decoration, or more than one bright accent at a time.

Page recipe

A page, in order.

Not every page needs every block, but they always come in this order.

  1. 01Hero

    Pill label, a big centred headline with one teal word, one plain line, the prompt box, Book a demo.

  2. 02The product

    A stand-in screen on the diamond grid, with two or three chips settling in round it.

  3. 03How it works

    Prepared, checked, approved, with the signature moment playing once.

  4. 04The proof

    A slow rail of the documents the agents prepare, labelled illustrative.

  5. 05The jobs

    Two or three panels: screen on one side, three plain points and the agent on the other.

  6. 06The stage

    The page’s one dark moment: film or texture behind big type.

  7. 07A voice

    Kane’s approved quote, or nothing.

  8. 08The close

    Pale diamond grid, one line, Book a demo.
