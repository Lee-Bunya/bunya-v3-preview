How it works

  1. 1Ask

    Say what you need in plain English, like “add the client’s preferred contact time to the client form”, or “an agent that chases missing documents before each review”.

  2. 2See it

    Bunya Shape builds it as a draft, on your own client record, for you to try.

  3. 3Approve

    Nothing goes live until someone on your team approves it. Or ask for it to be changed.

  4. 4Share

    Use forms and reports other practices have shared in the community library.
