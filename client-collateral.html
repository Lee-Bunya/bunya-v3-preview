Questions

Straight answers.

What is a Financial Services Guide?

The document every client must receive before you give advice. It explains who you are, the services you offer, how you’re paid and how to make a complaint.

Do you write the FSG?

We design it. The content comes from your current FSG and your licensee, who approves the wording before it’s final.

Do we need branding first?

Yes. The set is designed from your brand guidelines: logo files, fonts and colours. If you don’t have them, add a brand kit, guidelines or refresh.

How is this different from the Brand Document Pack?

Client Collateral is what a new client reads before advice. The Brand Document Pack is your SOAs, review packs and client documents.
