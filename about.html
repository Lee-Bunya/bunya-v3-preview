Why Bunya exists

More Australians need advice. There aren’t more advisers.

The old answer to a growing client list was to hire. That answer is harder every year. The firms that grow now will be the ones whose systems carry the prework, so their advisers can carry the advice.

Bunya is shaped around the day-to-day work of Australian advice practices: the file notes, the fact finds, the statements of advice and the handovers between the first meeting and implemented advice.

Questions

About Bunya AI. Straight answers.

What does Bunya AI do?

Bunya Command Centre is financial advice software for Australian advice firms. It helps take clients from first meeting to implemented advice, with AI preparing the work and advisers approving every step, inside your firm’s own Microsoft 365.

Who is Bunya for?

Established Australian advice practices with real client volume, where new-client advice waits on prework and the principal wants to grow without simply adding headcount. Most already work in Microsoft 365.

Who is Bunya not for?

Firms that only need a clean CRM, want AI to make advice decisions for them, or want software they can switch on the same afternoon.

Does Bunya make advice decisions?

No. AI prepares the work. Advisers keep strategy and product judgement, and a clickthrough is not advice approval.

Is Bunya AI an Australian company?

Yes. Bunya AI Pty Ltd (ABN 39 700 067 545) is an Australian company with an Australian team. You can reach us at info@bunya.ai or on 1300 286 922.
