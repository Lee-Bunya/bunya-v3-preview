Questions advisers ask first.

Where does our client data live?

In your firm’s own Microsoft 365. Client records, documents and approvals stay in your tenancy, and Bunya works with Teams, Outlook and SharePoint.

Does anything go to a client without a person?

No. Bunya prepares the work, and nothing is used or sent until someone on your team checks and approves it.

Do we have to replace everything at once?

No. Start with Command Centre, add only the features your firm needs, and keep the tools that already work for you.

What about our history?

Choose to start clean, bring your active clients across, or bring the full history. There’s a trial run before the real move.

We’re with a licensee. Is that a problem?

For statements of advice, we ask your licensee to approve the process first. Self-licensed firms can start straight away.
