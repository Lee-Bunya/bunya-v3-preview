Questions

Does Bunya write the SOA?

Yes. Bunya drafts the Statement of Advice in your template, and your adviser checks and approves it before it goes to the client. If your firm is self-licensed, you can start straight away. If you’re with a licensee, we’ll ask them to approve it first.

Do our paraplanners still have a role?

Yes. The draft can go to your paraplanner to refine, or straight to the adviser to check. Your firm decides the handover.

What does it need?

The fact find and Strategy and modelling, so both are added with it.

Is it live?
