It isn’t your team.

Your people are good. They’re careful, they know your clients, and they care about getting it right.

The problem is where the work lives.

The client’s details are in the CRM. The numbers are in a spreadsheet. The conversation is in someone’s inbox. The signed documents are in a shared drive. So every step of the advice starts with someone copying, chasing or searching.

You’ve probably tried to fix it by adding another tool. Most firms do. And each new tool becomes one more place to copy things into.

What actually fixes it is one place for all of it: one record for each client, from the first meeting to the review a year on, that does the copying for you.

That’s what we built. It’s called Bunya.

Here’s what next Thursday looks like.

At 4:05, your meeting ends. By the time you’re back at your desk, the file note is waiting, with the actions and the follow-up email, drafted from the Teams meeting you recorded with the client’s consent. You read it, change a line and approve it.

On Friday morning, the fact find is already filled in from the meeting, their super statements and the client portal. Each answer shows where it came from. One salary doesn’t match, so your team checks it before locking it. Nobody retyped a thing.

The modelling starts from the confirmed fact find, current and proposed side by side. You set the assumptions and choose the strategy. Then the SOA is drafted in your template, with every figure linked to where it came from, for your paraplanner to refine or for you to check and approve. The SOA generator is in pilot with early firms.

Before it goes out, the file is checked against your licensee’s standards and anything missing is flagged. That evening, your clients read their advice in their own portal, in your brand, and sign it on their phones. It’s filed to their record. No printing, no scanning, no chasing.

A year later, the review starts on time, from the client’s real dates. What’s changed is gathered before the meeting, and the fee consent renewal goes out with the pack once your team approves it.

And when anyone asks “where are we up to?”, the answer is on one screen.

That’s a firm using the whole Advice Engine. Yours can start with just the base and add pieces as it’s ready.

Notice what didn’t change: you still make every call. Bunya prepares the work. Nothing is used or sent until someone on your team checks it.
