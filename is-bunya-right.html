An honest fit check

Is Bunya right for your firm?

Bunya suits some advice firms better than others. Here’s how to tell which you are, before you book anything.

A good fit if…

  • Your firm works in Microsoft 365Outlook, Teams and SharePoint are already where your team works, or you’re ready to move there.
  • Work stalls between stepsActions, reviews, fee consents and SOAs wait because nobody can see whose turn it is.
  • Details get typed more than onceWhat a client says ends up retyped into the CRM, the fact find and the advice documents.
  • You want the records in your own tenancyClient records, documents and approvals in your firm’s own Microsoft 365, under your administration.
  • You want AI that prepares, not decidesAI prepares the work, and a person on your team approves every step before anything is used or sent.
  • You want it shaped around your firmSet up around your stages, roles and templates. Then your team can build its own apps and agents in plain English with Bunya Shape, no developer needed.

Probably not right yet if…

Better to know now than after a demo.

  • You’re not on Microsoft 365Command Centre runs in your firm’s own Microsoft 365. If you’re not on it and don’t plan to be, Bunya isn’t the right fit.
  • You want a free tool you can start todayBunya is set up for your firm, with a $7,249 standard setup and a $500 monthly minimum. A free, self-serve app will be quicker to start.
  • Meeting notes are your only problemIf everything after the note already runs well, a note tool may be enough. Here’s how to tell.
  • You need a particular connection on day oneConnections to platforms, modelling and research tools are assessed and agreed for your firm. None should be assumed, so ask before you commit.
  • Your licensee can’t review new systems yetIf you’re with a licensee, they approve Bunya first. If that can’t happen this year, it’s worth waiting.

Five questions. One answer.

Answer honestly. Nothing is sent anywhere.

  1. Does your team work in Microsoft 365 (Outlook and Teams)?

  2. Does work often wait between steps after a client meeting?

  3. Does your team type the same client details into more than one system?

  4. Do you want AI to prepare the work, with a person approving every step?

  5. Do you look after more than about 170 client households?

Common questions.

Do we need Microsoft 365 to use Bunya?

Yes. Command Centre runs in your firm’s own Microsoft 365, with structured records in Dataverse and documents in SharePoint. Microsoft bills its own licences directly.

Can we keep the software we use now?

Yes. Start with Command Centre, add only the pieces your firm needs, and keep the tools that already work for you. We look at what you use now and agree what to keep, connect or replace.

What if we’re with a licensee?

If your firm is self-licensed, you can start straight away. If you’re with a licensee, we ask them to approve Bunya first, with the Licensee Green-Light Pack, and we join the approval call.

Is there a minimum price?

Yes. The Bunya fee is at least $500 a month, whatever your size. Command Centre is from $3 per client household a month, and a couple counts as one household.

Can we see it before we decide?

Yes. The demo is one free meeting with no obligation: you see Command Centre with example data, and we talk through how your firm works. You can also build yours online to see your price first.

What does getting started cost?

Standard setup is $7,249 once. It includes setup in your Microsoft 365, moving your data across with a trial run first, training, and a named engineer beside you for 30 days after go-live, or 45 with the Advice Engine.
