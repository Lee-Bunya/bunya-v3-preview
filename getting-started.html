Questions

Straight answers.

Is the demo free?

Yes. The demo is one free meeting with no obligation.

Do we have to move all our history?

No. You can start clean, bring your active clients across, or bring the full history. A trial run comes before the real move.

We’re with a licensee. Does that change anything?

For statements of advice, yes. If your firm is self-licensed, you can start straight away. If you’re with a licensee, we’ll ask them to approve it first.

What does setup cost?

Standard setup is $7,249, once, and it’s included if you buy Command Centre outright. Microsoft licences and usage are part of the cost picture too, and are set out before you start. Draft prices, A$ ex GST.

What happens after go-live?

Close support in the first weeks, video tutorials for each role, and a regular session to choose the next improvement. Care, our support and updates, is included when you subscribe.
