03 · Plan an evaluation

A clear decision.
Not a leap of faith.

Work from your own workflows and data, and agree what changes before committing to anything.

  1. Name the bottleneck

    Follow a recent new client from first meeting to implemented advice. Note where work waited, who did the prework and what had to be re-entered.

  2. Inventory what the firm relies on

    List records, documents, templates, modelling, research, reports, integrations and any licensee requirements. Identify who owns each requirement.

  3. Agree what stays and what changes

    Map each requirement to a demonstrated capability, a separately scoped connection or a tool you retain. Do not assume Bunya covers every Xplan module.

  4. Test with sample information

    See the proposed workflow with illustrative or authorised sample data, including the review and approval steps and what happens when information is missing.

  5. Plan any change in stages

    If records need to move, confirm export rights and formats, validate a sample and agree training, reconciliation and support. Staging depends on technical and contractual feasibility.

04 · Common questions

Know the scope.
Understand the trade-offs.

A useful comparison ends with clear responsibilities, costs and a practical next step.

Does Bunya replace Xplan?

[CONFIRM wording for sign-off] Not like-for-like. Bunya Command Centre focuses on the advice delivery journey, from first meeting to implemented advice. Xplan covers a broader set of capabilities, including modelling and research tools that Command Centre does not set out to replicate. Many firms may keep Xplan or other specialist tools; the Blueprint identifies what to retain, connect or replace.

Can Bunya connect directly to our Xplan site?

Do not assume a ready-made connection. Access, available interfaces, permissions and commercial arrangements need to be confirmed with the relevant providers before an integration can be scoped.

Could we move history and documents later?

If your firm decides to move any records, the answer depends on export access, formats, record quality, document storage and mapping. Assess and validate a sample before agreeing the scope. Some items may need a different retention or access approach.

Is Bunya cheaper?

There is no universal answer, and the two do different jobs. Compare actual proposals over the same period, including implementation, licences, usage, support, retained software and internal effort. Do not assume time saved becomes a cash saving.

Do we have to change everything at once?

No. Firms can start with the workflow that is most backed up. Any staged change must account for data ownership, duplicate entry, integrations, licensing and support, and the sequence is agreed after assessing your setup.

What does “your own Microsoft 365 environment” mean?

Command Centre is delivered in your firm’s Microsoft 365 environment. Ownership terms, supplied components, documentation and handover are defined in the contract. Microsoft and third-party licensing and agreed support remain ongoing considerations.
