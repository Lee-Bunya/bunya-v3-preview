Questions

Straight answers.

01What’s the difference between an agent and an app?
An agent does one advice job inside Command Centre, such as a file note or review prep. An app is a separate workspace beside it for the rest of running a practice: marketing, new business, your team, your books and your logins.
02Do we need any apps to deliver advice?
No. Command Centre and its agents deliver advice. Apps are optional, and your firm adds the ones that fit.
03Where do apps keep their data?
In your firm’s own Microsoft 365, alongside Command Centre. [CONFIRM: data location for each app]
04Can we add an app later?
Yes. Apps are switched on per firm, and the ones you choose are agreed in your Blueprint.
