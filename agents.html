Made to work with the core.

Every agent works from the client record, and puts its draft back there with its sources linked.
A named person checks each draft. Nothing moves on until they approve it, and the approval stays on the record.
Email, meetings and documents give each agent its context, inside your firm’s own tenancy.

Questions

Straight answers.

01What is a Bunya agent?
An agent does one advice job inside Bunya Command Centre. It gathers what it needs from the client record, prepares a draft, and waits for a named person on your team to approve it.
02Do agents make decisions?
No. Agents prepare and flag. Advice, approvals and anything sent to a client stay with your people, and every approval is kept on the record.
03Which agents come with Command Centre?
Your Blueprint sets which agents are switched on for your firm. [CONFIRM: which agents are included and which are extras]
04Where do agents run?
Inside your firm’s own Microsoft 365, next to the client records they work from. [CONFIRM: where AI processing happens]
