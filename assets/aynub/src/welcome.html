About Aynub Financial

A name that turns around.

The bunya pine lives for centuries and drops its big cones every year, with especially heavy crops about every three years. It grows on a long timeline, and so does good advice. We turned its name around because that’s how we plan: start with the life you want, then work backwards to what you do today.

Aynub Financial is a fee-for-service advice practice based in Kingaroy, in the South Burnett, near the Bunya Mountains. No bank or fund owns us, we charge our fees in dollars and we accept no commissions, so we have no product to push. We work with individuals, families and business owners, and we act in your best interests at all times, in line with the Corporations Act and the Financial Planners and Advisers Code of Ethics.

Our mark, the Seed Mirror, is two bunya seeds leaning into each other like a reflection on still water: you and your adviser. Where they overlap, warm and cool mix into the shared plan. The gold seed above is the future you’re planning toward.

We bring super, retirement, investments, personal insurance, cash flow and debt, aged care and estate planning together in one plan. Where you need specialist help beyond our scope, such as legal advice or complex tax, we work alongside your accountant, solicitor or other trusted professionals so you get the right support.

Your identification documents

Verifying your identity before we can act.

Because we arrange financial products for clients, we’re required to identify you and verify your identity under the Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006 before we do so. Where possible we verify your details electronically; otherwise, our process usually means providing either:

Option one1

One current photographic document

An Australian passport, an Australian driver licence, or a state or territory proof of age card.

or
Option two2

Non-photographic documents

If you don’t have photo ID, we’ll confirm which combination we can accept. This may be a full birth certificate or citizenship certificate, together with a recent document showing your current residential address, such as a utility or council rates notice.

You can bring the originals to your discovery meeting for us to sight, or upload clear colour scans through the Client Portal. We record the details we need (your name, date of birth and address, the document type and number, and how we verified it) rather than keeping full copies of your documents. Please don’t send identification as an unencrypted email attachment.

Partners and entities

We’ll need identification for each of you and, where we act for your self-managed super fund, for the fund and its trustee as well.

Certified copies

Who can certify your documents

If we ask you for certified copies, for example because we can’t sight your originals or verify them electronically, a certifier sights the original, then writes on the copy that it is a true copy of the document they have seen, and signs and dates it with their name, their occupation and, where they have one, their registration or membership number. They cannot certify their own documents, and they should not certify for a close family member.

Examples of people who can usually certify · please check with us first

  • a Justice of the Peace, or a bail justice
  • a legal practitioner, a notary public, or a judge, magistrate, registrar or deputy registrar of a court
  • a police officer
  • an accountant who is a member of CPA Australia, Chartered Accountants ANZ or the Institute of Public Accountants with at least two years of continuous membership
  • an officer of a bank, building society, credit union or finance company with at least two years of continuous service, or an agent of the Australian Postal Corporation who supplies postal services to the public
  • a permanent employee of Australia Post with at least two years of continuous service
  • a medical practitioner, dentist, pharmacist, nurse, optometrist, physiotherapist, chiropractor, psychologist or veterinary surgeon
  • a full-time teacher at a school or tertiary education institution
  • a member of the Commonwealth, a State or a Territory Parliament, or of a local government authority
  • an Australian consular officer or diplomatic officer, if you are overseas

If none of these is convenient

Some Australia Post outlets and pharmacies may certify documents; check availability and any fee first. Call us on (07) 5550 1240 if you’re unsure whether someone qualifies, or if you’re overseas and none of these options is available to you.

Privacy

How we protect your information.

Aynub Financial is bound by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. This statement explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the controls we place around it.

What we collect

To give advice that meets the best interests duty we need a detailed picture of your circumstances. That typically includes your name, date of birth and contact details; identification documents; your income, spending, assets, debts and super; existing insurance policies and investments; your goals, timeframes and attitude to risk; and, where it is directly relevant to a recommendation, health and family information. We collect this from you through the forms in this pack and our meetings, and from third parties where you have authorised us to ask.

How we use it

We use your information to prepare, present, implement and review your advice, and to meet our obligations under the Corporations Act, the AML/CTF Act and taxation law. We also retain it to meet the record-keeping requirements that apply to financial advice businesses. We do not sell your information, and we do not make it available to third parties for marketing.

Who we share it with

We disclose only what is necessary, and only to parties involved in delivering your advice: product and platform providers, insurers, fund managers, our compliance and audit providers, and our own professional advisers, together with your accountant or solicitor where you have asked us to work with them. Some providers may store data outside Australia; where that is the case we take reasonable steps to satisfy ourselves that comparable protections apply. We will not disclose your information to anyone else without your consent unless the law compels us to.

How we protect it

Your records are held in access-controlled systems protected by multi-factor authentication, and access is limited to the team members who need it to do their work. Paper documents are stored securely, digitised and then destroyed once they are no longer needed. We keep each type of record for the period the law sets for it: advice records generally for seven years, and identity-verification records for seven years after our relationship with you ends. In line with the 2026 AML/CTF reforms, we keep the identity details and how we verified them, not full copies of your identity documents. After that, records are securely destroyed or de-identified. If a data breach occurs that is likely to cause you serious harm, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Accessing or correcting your information

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, and to have anything inaccurate corrected, at any time. Write to Mara Holloway at mara@aynub.com and we will respond within 30 days.

If you’re unhappy

If you are unhappy with the way we have handled your information, please tell us first so that we can put it right. Our Complaints Policy at aynub.com/complaints explains how we handle every complaint. If you remain dissatisfied, you can refer the matter to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au, or to the Australian Financial Complaints Authority (AFCA).
