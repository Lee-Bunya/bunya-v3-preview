01Concessional contributions
Strategy 01 · Concessional contributions

Press the personal deductible contribution strategy forward.

Recommended FY26 contribution
$25,000

$21,250 invested after 15% contributions tax; about $8,000 less income tax at Oliver’s 32% rate.

Why this matters

Last year’s $40,000 contribution drew on carry-forward cap. For FY26 the cap is $30,000 and employer super uses about $14,880. Oliver’s total super balance was about $321,000 on 30 June 2025, under $500,000, so about $54,000 of unused cap can be carried forward, including $6,820 from 2020–21 that expires after 30 June 2026.

What we’re recommending

Pay $25,000 from bank savings so Banksia Super receives it by 30 June 2026 (FY26 total $39,880). Lodge a Notice of Intent and get Banksia Super’s acknowledgement before claiming the deduction. From 1 July 2026, salary sacrifice $15,000 a year, with employer super $29,880, inside the $32,500 cap for 2026–27.

NoteDivision 293 tax doesn’t apply: income plus concessional contributions is well under $250,000. We confirm caps and carry-forward in ATO online services first.

Strategy 04 · Protection review

Refresh your insurance design inside super.

Why this matters

Oliver’s cover in Banksia Super is $250,000 Death, $125,000 TPD and $5,400/month Income Protection (2-year benefit, 60-day wait). With a $112,000 home loan and three children, TPD looks light, and a 2-year IP benefit leaves a gap if a serious condition keeps him out of work longer. It hasn’t been reviewed since it began.

What we’re recommending

Run a fresh needs analysis covering Death, TPD and Income Protection, sized to the loan, living costs and the children’s needs. Consider increasing TPD and extending the IP benefit period to age 65, balanced against the premium cost of Oliver’s occupation rating.

NotePremiums (about $2,160 a year) come from the super balance. Any change is separate advice; we’ll confirm whether a Record of Advice or a new SOA is required.

Good to know

Things to be aware of.

Six practical points
01ROA or a new SOA

A Record of Advice works only if your circumstances and the basis of advice haven’t changed significantly since your SOA. If they have, a new SOA is needed.

02Concessional cap check

Carry-forward needs a total super balance under $500,000 on 30 June 2025. Oliver’s was about $321,000. The $30,000 standard cap has no such test.

03Notice of Intent timing

Give Banksia Super the Notice of Intent, and get its acknowledgement, before the earlier of lodging your FY26 return or 30 June 2027, and before any rollover.

04Insurance underwriting

Any increase to TPD or extension of IP cover may require updated medical underwriting, modelled in the separate insurance advice.

05Investment market timing

Cash redeployment trades will be sequenced to manage market entry, not lumped on a single day.

06Fee consent window

Your reference day is 12 May 2026. Renewal consent can be given from 13 March and must be given by 9 October 2026, or the arrangement ends and fees stop.
