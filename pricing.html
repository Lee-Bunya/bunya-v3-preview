How getting live works · Is Bunya right for you?

Questions

Straight answers.

What counts as an account?

A couple counts as one account, with the trusts, SMSFs and companies they own.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. The Bunya fee is at least $500 a month, whatever your size.

Do bigger firms pay less?

Yes. Every account above 1,000 is 16% less.

Why are apps a separate line?

Apps are a flat price a month for your firm, however many accounts you have, so they never change your price per account.

What if we write our own SOAs?

Start with Command Centre on its own. If you need one drafted, we draft it for $153 an SOA.

What do client reviews cost?

On Command Centre, each client review is 97c. With the whole Advice Engine, every review is included.

Can we change our Bunya later?

Yes. Add or remove features and apps as your firm changes. New features are offered, never forced.

Can we own it instead?

Yes. Command Centre is $104,020 once, or $178,321 with the Advice Engine, with setup included, then support and updates are $1,497 a month. See both prices under Buy it outright.

What does Microsoft charge?

Microsoft bills its own licences directly: Power Apps Premium about $29.90 a person a month, AI credits from about $50 a month (an SOA draft uses about $6), and Teams Phone about $12 a person a month if you use Bunya Voice. Every Microsoft cost is listed above.

What support is included?

Premium support comes with every Bunya: email support with a reply within 24 to 48 hours. Priority support, with a 1 to 3 hour turnaround, is $1,997 a month.
