How getting live works · Is Bunya right for you?

Questions

Straight answers.

What counts as a household?

A couple counts as one household, with the trusts, SMSFs and companies they own.

Is there a minimum?

Yes. The Bunya fee is at least $500 a month, whatever your size.

Do bigger firms pay less?

Yes. Every household above 1,000 is 16% less.

Why are apps a separate line?

Apps are a flat price a month for your firm, however many households you have, so they never change your price per household.

What if we write our own SOAs?

Start with Command Centre on its own. If you need one drafted, we draft it for $153 an SOA.

What do client reviews cost?

On Command Centre, each client review is 97c. With the whole Advice Engine, every review is included.

Can we change our Bunya later?

Yes. Add or remove features and apps as your firm changes. New features are offered, never forced.

Can we own it instead?

Yes. Command Centre is $104,020 once, or $178,321 with the Advice Engine, with setup included, then Care is $1,497 a month. Choose it when you build yours.

What does Microsoft charge?

Microsoft bills its own licences directly. Power Apps Premium is about $29.90 a person a month, and AI usage is about $6 an SOA draft.
