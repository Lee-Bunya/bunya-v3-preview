Command Centre

What it is, and what comes with it.

What is Bunya?

Bunya AI builds Bunya Command Centre, the practice operating system for Australian financial advice firms. It prepares the file notes, fact finds, SOAs and review packs from first meeting to implemented advice, and your team approves every step, inside your firm’s own Microsoft 365.

What comes with Command Centre?

The client record, the advice pipeline and practice reports, plus Meeting Notes, Review Packs with fee consent (97c a client review), Care (our support and updates) and Bunya Shape. See Command Centre

What can we add?

The Advice Engine, which adds the fact find, insurance needs, modelling and every SOA drafted for you, and five apps: Client Portal, Broadcast HQ, Field Notes, Revenue Import and Bunya Voice. Brand, documents, website and marketing are there too. Pick only what your firm needs. Build yours

Does Bunya write the Statement of Advice?

Yes. Bunya drafts the Statement of Advice in your template, and your adviser checks and approves it before it goes to the client. If your firm is self-licensed, you can start straight away. If you’re with a licensee, we’ll ask them to approve it first. With the Advice Engine every SOA is included; on Command Centre, each SOA we draft for you is $153. See the Advice Engine

How is it different from a CRM?

Client records are part of the foundation. Command Centre is built around the work between the first meeting and implemented advice: file notes, fact finds, statements of advice, reviews and approvals, with AI preparing that work for your team to check.

Do we have to replace the system we use now?

No. Start with Command Centre, add only the pieces your firm needs, and keep the tools that already work for you. We look at what your firm uses now and agree what to keep, connect or replace.

Does it connect to our platforms and modelling tools?

Command Centre is built in Microsoft 365, including SharePoint and Teams. Connections to other platforms, modelling or research tools are assessed and agreed for your firm; none should be assumed. Your existing tools can stay where they are.

AI and approval

AI prepares the work. People make the calls.

What does the AI actually do?

It uses the client’s own information to prepare the work between steps: file notes, actions, fact-find inputs, draft statements of advice, review packs and follow-up emails. Your team checks it before anything is used or sent.

Can AI change records or send things without us?

No. AI-prepared work waits for a person on your team to approve it. Advice, client emails and marketing are reviewed before they’re used or sent, and Broadcast HQ only publishes versions your team has approved.

Does AI replace our professional judgement?

No. AI prepares and supports the work. Your team stays responsible for reviewing it and making professional decisions. Advisers keep strategy and product judgement, and a clickthrough is never advice approval.

Can we see where AI was used, and who approved it?

AI-prepared work is marked as AI-prepared, and approvals stay with the client record they belong to. Which events are recorded, and for how long, is agreed for your firm.

Are client meetings recorded with consent?

Yes. The adviser tells clients at the start, and Teams shows a recording banner. Meeting Notes covers Teams meetings and Teams calls; Field Notes covers meetings in person, away from the office.

Security and your data

The short version. The full page

Where are our records and documents kept?

In your firm’s own Microsoft 365: structured records in Dataverse, and documents and conversation records in SharePoint. Data location, retention and any connected AI or third-party processing are reviewed as part of the solution design.

Is our data used to train AI models?

The AI services used, and their data-use terms, are reviewed with your firm as part of the solution design.

Can different team members have different access?

Yes. Roles and permissions are set around the work each person needs to do, then configured and tested for your firm before go-live.

Can we move data from our current system?

Yes. Migration starts with an assessment of your records, export options, duplicates, documents and permissions. We then agree the mapping, checks and scope. The effort depends on the quality of the data you’re moving from.

How do you handle training and go-live?

Go-live happens in releases. Each release is tested on your own work and accepted by your team. The Team Quick-Start Kit gives every role short videos, a one-page guide and live sessions, and a named engineer stays beside you for 30 days after go-live, or 45 with the Advice Engine.

What happens when we need help or want changes?

Care, our support and updates, is included when you subscribe. Changes are scoped and tested before they’re released to your team. For a named engineer and a one-hour response, Care Premium is $1,997 a month.

Can we change Bunya ourselves?

Yes. With Bunya Shape, included with every Bunya, you ask in plain English for changes to your forms, reports and screens, or for new apps and agents of your own. Bunya Shape builds it on your own client record, you try it first, and nothing goes live until you approve it. For bigger builds, our build team does it with you, and your first month of Bunya Build is included. See Bunya Shape

Will new features change how we work?

Only if you want them. New features are offered, never forced: your firm adds them when it’s ready. Fixes and improvements to what you already use come with Care, and Microsoft updates its own platform on its schedule.
